SCIENTIFIC CORRECTIONS
The old environment omitted 000, limited neighbors to a resolution sphere and a zone slab,
and dropped neighbors exactly on the cutoff. It also silently capped integer indices.
The new environment enumerates every nonzero integer offset Δhkl with |BΔhkl| ≤ r_cut.
Each neighbor is target hkl + Δhkl, independently of display filters and viewport.
000 is therefore included whenever eligible. Its contribution is shown separately.
Boundary tolerance is 64 machine epsilons × max(1, r_cut) Å⁻¹.
No structure factors, intensity proxies, normalized sum, contribution floor, or extra score terms.
B = A⁻ᵀ (no 2π); radial excitation is |k0 + Bh| − |k0|, evaluated in a stable rationalized form.
Direct-coordinate [uvw] maps through A before normalization. Nonorthogonal cells and tilted
orientations were checked explicitly; positive scaling of [uvw] leaves scores unchanged.
One configuration supplies startup and reset defaults: s0=0.005, sigma_C=0.10, r_cut=0.30 Å⁻¹;
optional multiplication by E(g) is OFF. These are exploratory, uncalibrated parameters.

|s_g| ≤ s_display, and 1/lambda + g_Z > 0. Defaults: |g| ≤ 2 and |s_g| ≤ 0.008 Å⁻¹.

Synthetic minimum intensity, resolution attenuation, and percentile normalization were removed.
Any safely representable nonzero integer hkl can be inspected outside these filters.

The 000 reference can be hidden without affecting scoring. Neighbor tables always show eligible 000.
Tables can be sorted and expanded to all rows. Any omitted contribution sum is reported.
Automatic color scaling uses 0 to maximum displayed absolute score and is explicitly labeled.
A locked scale retains numeric limits during orientation and display changes.
Lab X/Y crystal tilts use a fixed detector frame; canvas gestures change only the viewport.
