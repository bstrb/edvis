Surface-Pivot Rocking Sphere

The fixed point in space is the attachment point on the sphere surface. The rod is an internal radius from that surface point to the sphere center, and its rocking directions fill a cone whose apex is on the surface and whose open end points inward. Because the center moves, the sphere itself sweeps a nontrivial path rather than simply rotating in place.
Geometry. The teal point is the fixed attachment point in space. The blue rod runs from that point to the sphere center, so its length is the sphere radius. Rocking changes both the position and the orientation of the sphere.
